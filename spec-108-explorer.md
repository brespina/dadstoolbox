# Break: spec-108 explorer

I would love to spend a slow afternoon learning to letter with a broad-nib pen.
Just a cheap pen, a pad of smooth paper and a printed alphabet sheet. I'd fill pages with
the same few letters until the thick and thin strokes started to look deliberate.

After a morning of reading how a signature becomes a PNG on a PDF page, I'd like to make
something with ink that no one will ever scan, flatten or embed.
