I like how a QR code still reads when every square is a circle.
The scanner only checks the middle of each square, so all that
rounding is just for people. It's a nice kind of slack: a strict
machine format that still leaves room to make it look friendly.

Signed distance fields are another small joy. One line, 0.5 - d,
and a circle's edge goes from stair-steps to smooth. On a break I'd
make a little field of soft dots and watch them merge into blobs
as their radii grow, just to see what it looks like.
