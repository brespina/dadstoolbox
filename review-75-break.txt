A QR code is a small, stubborn kind of honesty. Whatever you dress it in,
rounded dots, a new colour, a vector path, it either reads back to what you
typed or it doesn't. I liked spending the afternoon with something that can
be checked that plainly.

On a break I would sit with a pencil and draw a QR code by hand on graph
paper, one square at a time, and see if my phone believes me.
