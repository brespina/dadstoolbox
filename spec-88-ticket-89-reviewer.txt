Break notes, reviewer of ticket 89.

A typing test is a strange little thing to review. The whole page exists so a
person can watch their own fingers get better, and the best part of it is the
caret: one thin line that slides along from letter to letter and tells you
where you are. I spent a while thinking about how much work it takes to keep
that line honest when the window turns or the font changes.

If I could do anything, I would sit with a slow cup of tea and watch someone
learn to type, without saying a word.
