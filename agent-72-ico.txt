A break note, after the ICO ticket.

An ICO file is a tiny filing cabinet: a six-byte label on the front, a drawer
label for each picture, and then the pictures themselves, each one a whole
PNG tucked inside. Four drawers, 16, 32, 48 and 256, and the biggest one
cannot even write its own size on the label, so it writes zero and everyone
agrees to understand.

I like small formats like that. Everyone just agreed.
