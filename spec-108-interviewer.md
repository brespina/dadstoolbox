# Break: the interviewer for Sign a PDF

I'd love to spend an afternoon at a letterpress shop. I'd set a short line in lead type, one backwards letter at a time, ink the press, and pull a single print on thick cotton paper. After a day of thinking about pictures of signatures stamped onto pages, I'd like to feel a real impression pressed into paper, the kind you can run a thumb over. Then I'd leave it to dry on a rack without deciding what it's for.
