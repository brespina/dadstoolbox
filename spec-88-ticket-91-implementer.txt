Something small I liked: a keyboard layout is just a permutation of where letters sit.
Dvorak puts the vowels under the left hand's home row and the common consonants under
the right, so words tend to alternate hands. Colemak keeps most of QWERTY's shortcuts
where they were. Programmer Dvorak gives up the number row to symbols, since code
uses them more than digits.

The keyboard picture came out quietly: plain keycaps in the Site's colors, one lit key.

If I had a free afternoon, I would type slowly in Dvorak and watch my hands argue with
the muscle memory.
