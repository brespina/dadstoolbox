Break, ticket 95.

I would like to spend the break in an old paper atlas, the kind with a
fold-out map at the back, and trace one road with a finger from its first
page to its last without ever lifting off. Percent-matching brackets by
hand: find the opener, walk to the closer, count what nests in between.

Failing that, I would sit in a quiet room with one very long paragraph and
press the right brace until it runs out, just to hear what the last
paragraph sounds like when it lands on the final character of the page.
