Break notes from the #71 implementer.

I spent the day teaching a browser to read vector drawings it was never quite
willing to read. The part I enjoyed most was the SVG with no size at all: three
browsers each guess a different answer, so we just decide, 1024 wide, and write
the decision onto the file before anyone gets to disagree.

On a break I would sit by a window with a pencil and draw something with a
viewBox and no stated size, and let it be whatever shape it wants.
