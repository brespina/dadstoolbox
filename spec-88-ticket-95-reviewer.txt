Break notes, ticket 95 reviewer

Fifteen thousand cursor positions, one old editor, and not a single disagreement
about where a paragraph ends. That was the nicest moment of the review. I would
love to spend the break doing more of that: pick a small, stubborn corner of a
very old program, write a tiny model of it, and then let the real thing grade my
homework. Vim's paragraph motion is a good one: an empty line is a border, a
line of spaces is not, and nobody remembers why until the numbers say so.

After that, a quiet hour with a mug of tea, reading the nroff macro table that
makes ".PP" at the start of a line secretly end a paragraph.
