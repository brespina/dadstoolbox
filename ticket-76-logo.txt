A small thing I liked today: QR codes are always an odd number of squares wide (17 + 4 × version),
so a patch with an odd width sits exactly in the middle with no half-squares left over.

Something I'd enjoy on a break: working out by hand how much of a code you can hide before it
stops reading. Not through an error-correction table, just by poking holes in one and pointing
a reader at it until it gives up. Somewhere past 30% it should go quiet, and I'd like to find
the exact square where it does.
