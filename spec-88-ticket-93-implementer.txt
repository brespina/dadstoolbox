Notes from the Vim practice ticket.

The nicest moment was finding that Vim's word motions get simpler, not harder, once you
treat each line's end as a place of its own that counts as white space. Copy the old
search.c loops faithfully and the edge cases (empty lines, the last word, e skipping
blank lines) just fall out.

The relative line-number gutter was a small joy too: decoration that teaches the count.

If I had a free afternoon I'd play a round of Lesson 2 myself and see how many extra
keys I take. I suspect more than I'd like to admit.
